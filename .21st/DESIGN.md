## Brand & Style
The design system for **ForgeRoom** is engineered for high-performance real-time AI collaboration, blending enterprise-grade reliability with the fluid momentum of live multiplayer workspaces. The visual theme is **Dark-Tech Glassmorphism** with **Dynamic Neon Energy**. It features deep dark backdrops layered with semi-transparent charcoal panels, accentuated by a mix of 5 vibrant neon colors to signify different team members and active AI nodes.

## Color Guide
- **Midnight Canvas (#07090e)**: The deep, dark base to reduce fatigue.
- **Electric Teal (#00E5FF)**: Signals real-time synchronization, active cursors, and "live" moments.
- **Hyper Violet (#8B5CF6)**: Used for secondary actions, AI insights, and player presence.
- **Neon Amber (#FFA726)**: Represents human team interaction (cursors, mentions, warnings).
- **Crimson Pink (#FF2E93)**: High-energy accent color used for secondary user cursors and particle streams.
- **Grid Emerald (#10B981)**: Indicates successful processes and green metrics in comparison.

## Typography
- **Geist**: Used for primary headers, tags, labels, and table headers to evoke technical precision.
- **Inter**: Used for body copy, paragraphs, and list details for high readability against dark themes.
- **JetBrains Mono**: Used for coding sessions, console outputs, and system log streams.
